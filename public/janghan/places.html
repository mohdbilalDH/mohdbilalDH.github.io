Places in 長恨

Which places does the magazine name, in what spatial function, and in whose texts? This is the full record of coded place evidence — including the evidence that cannot be drawn on a map.

The place register

53 counted mentions across 20 places. Rows are ordered by how often a place is mentioned — an ordering, not a measure of importance.

Which places does 『長恨』 name, in what spatial function, and in whose texts?
Dataset
place_mentions (dataset v1.2) · place ontology v1.1 · p30_discourse_class v1 · text items (v3.3-freeze+v3.3.1)
Witness basis

Uncertainty
Every mention carries its status. Nothing coded is hidden: records that were logged, excluded by rule, or rejected as false positives are listed in full below the register.
Issue 1 only. Every mention on this page comes from 창간호 (January 1927). 2월호 has not been coded for place, so it contributes nothing here — its absence is a gap in the work, not a finding about the magazine. Nothing on this page should be read as describing 『長恨』 as a whole.
Why 33 of 53 mentions cannot appear on a map. Of the 20 places named in the coded evidence, 10 are points that can be located; the rest are countries, regions, a homeland and one venue whose 1927 location is not documented. A country is not a point, and 조선 — the most-mentioned place here — is not a location at all in most of its uses. Being unmappable is a property of the referent, not a measure of how much the place matters. A small locator map for the 10 mappable places is planned; it is not built yet, and it will be able to carry only 20 of these 53 mentions.

14 Chosŏn (조선/朝鮮) 조선; 朝鮮; 우리 조선 not a mappable point ▸

P30 · geographic_country / colonial_homeland · place record

Korea under Japanese rule (1910-45). The corpus speaks from inside it; recurring first-person-plural references (우리 조선) are analytically central to the colonial/spatial argument.

조선 is not plotted, and is not a point. It is the most-mentioned place in the coded evidence (14 of 53 counted mentions) and it is never a location on a map: it appears as a homeland frame, as a term of comparison, and as a standard someone is told to meet. What this does not show. That how often a place is named measures its importance to the magazine. That unmappable places are lesser evidence — they are 33 of 53 mentions, including the most-mentioned place of all. That 조선 is a geographic point; it is never plotted, and its uses are shown separately for that reason. That the number of places in a text says anything about mobility, spatial awareness or outlook. No such measure is computed here. That this describes the magazine: 13 catalogued places are never mentioned in Issue 1, and Issue 2 is uncoded.
