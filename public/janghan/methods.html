Methods & data

The corpus is versioned and frozen in stages, so that every figure can be traced to a dataset state and a git commit.

Corpus construction

The edition is built at three levels — bibliographic entry (an external catalogue record), editorial unit (a titled item as the magazine's apparatus presents it), and textual item (a continuous text with its own title or byline, and the primary coding unit). A symposium is one editorial unit with three nested responses; a poem printed inside an essay is a nested textual item.

Counts are reported at the editorial-unit level and always name their level. The reconstructed counts are not claims about a historically true number: they converge with the other witnesses after reconciliation, and the residual disagreements are published above.

Versions

  • v3.3-freeze — Issue 1 frozen: 52 textual items / 48 editorial units. Editorial-unit existence, boundaries and inclusion decisions are fixed; the poem layer stays amendable.
  • v3.3.1 — metadata-only amendment layer (one byline verified against the 1927 scan).
  • v3.4-issue2-reconciled — Issue 2: 46 textual items / 41 editorial units, working layer.
  • Place ontology v1.1 (33 places) · Issue-1 place dataset v1.2 · Stage 4 voice measures (Issue 1).

Coding

Place mentions are coded per episode — a continuous stretch in which one place functions in one dominant way — under an eight-value function taxonomy, with explicit rules for relational anchors, rhetorical macro-geography, and tabular data (excluded, but preserved as evidence). Statuses distinguish counted mentions from gap-logged, logged-not-counted, excluded-evidence and false-positive records, so that decisions are reproducible rather than invisible.

A 20% stratified audit of the Issue-1 coding measured recall at 82% before correction and precision at 100%; all misses traced to one mechanical defect (search-form coverage), which was then repaired and re-run.

Status of analysis. Issue-1 descriptive coding is complete; Issue-2 place and voice coding has not begun. Competing explanations for the observed patterns — genre composition, text length, page register, observer convention, retrieval error, editorial mediation, script register, and genuine expressive difference — remain under test. None is presented here as settled.

Datasets

  • text_items.json — Every textual item in both issues with ids, pages, attribution, register
  • editorial_units.json — Editorial units and their member items
  • contributors.json — Bylines, items, cross-issue links
  • places.json — Place ontology v1.1 (33 places)
  • place_mentions.json — Issue-1 place coding, all statuses
  • bibliographic_entries.json — Yonsei catalogue witnesses
  • speaker_turns.json — Round-table speaker turns
  • versions.json — Dataset versions and rights notes
