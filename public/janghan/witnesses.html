Witnesses & concordance

Four independent witnesses attest the contents of 『長恨』. None is treated as ground truth. Where they disagree, this edition records the disagreement rather than resolving it silently.

W1 · 1927 original scan

2005 digitisation of 창간호 (128 pp.): covers, the original table of contents, contributor photographs, the two-register layout and the colophon. Issue 1 only.

W2 · 2009 보고사 자료편

Re-typeset transcription with its own article numbering; spacing modernised, illegible passages marked □. Sole text witness for Issue 2.

W3 · Yonsei catalogue

Serial-article records: 49 entries for Issue 1, 44 for Issue 2. Counts poems and short items separately.

W4 · Published bibliography

김영희 2014 / 신현규 2010: 48 + 40 articles. Issue-level only — never attested per item, which is why its column is hatched throughout.

Rights. The 2009 edition carries ⓒ 손종흠·박경우·유춘동 2009 with an explicit no-reproduction notice; the acquisition terms of the 1927 scan are not yet recorded. Page images and full transcription are rights-gated; this edition publishes metadata, structure, coded evidence and short quotation.

V1 · Attestation concordance

Two units, used throughout this edition. An editorial unit is an item as the magazine itself presents it — one article, one column, or one prompt with its answers. A textual item is a single continuous text with its own title or byline: each answer to a prompt, and each poem printed inside someone else's article, is its own textual item. One editorial unit can therefore contain several textual items, which is why counts differ depending on which is being counted.

Which witnesses attest each item?

Each row is one reconstructed textual item; the three squares record which witnesses attest it. The right-hand column appears only where the Yonsei catalogue divides the magazine differently from this edition.

What this does not show. That any witness column is correct — the columns are sources, not verdicts. That items without a catalogue entry are errors: three Issue-2 items are attested by the 2009 edition alone and are flagged as provenance conflicts. That 48 / 49 / 44 / 40 are rival truth-claims; they are different counting units, chiefly over poems and prompted features.
