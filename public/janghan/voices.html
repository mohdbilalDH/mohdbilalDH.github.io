Voices

Who speaks in 『長恨』, and in what register. These measures describe texts, not people.

Exploratory — do not read as findings. Script density and politeness ratio are text-level measures. Within-contributor profiles proved unstable across issues (one pen name moves from 0.00 to 1.00; an unsigned recurring column moves 0.76 → 0.00 in the same genre), so these variables cannot serve as author fingerprints. Voice-mode is machine-assisted and validated at 75% against human reading. Issue-2 voice coding is not complete, and no cross-issue comparison has been run.
