Registers of writing

How much Chinese character text does each piece carry, and how formal is its address to the reader? These are measures of texts, not of the people credited with them.

Exploratory — not findings. Character density and politeness are text-level measures. Profiles of the same pen name proved unstable across the two issues — one moves from wholly plain to wholly polite, and an unsigned recurring column shifts just as far within the same genre. They therefore cannot serve as authorial fingerprints, and nothing here is offered as a conclusion about who wrote what. The reading mode is machine-assisted and agreed with a human reading in nine of twelve checks. Issue-2 measures are partial, and no comparison between the issues has been run.

Looking for the people? The bylines and their evidence are on Contributors.
