What this does not show. That the reconstruction supersedes the other two — it is a fourth reading, made from them. That the 1927 list is what the magazine contained: a table of contents is itself an editorial selection. That an unmatched catalogue entry is a cataloguing mistake, or that an unmatched item is an intruder.
