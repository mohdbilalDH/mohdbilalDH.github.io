What this does not show. That a shared pseudonym is a shared person. Only two of these threads rest on explicit serial numbering (一 / 二). The rest are bylines that read alike; the line style is the claim, and it must not be flattened. That the unsigned recurring column has a known author — the rat narrator returns, but nothing identifies who wrote either instance. That the number of appearances measures a contributor’s standing in the magazine. That contributors appearing once were less involved: the magazine ran for two issues, so a single appearance may simply be all there was time for.
