# Cover images

`jh01-cover-emuseum.jpg` and `jh02-cover-emuseum.jpg` are photographs of the two physical 1927
issues of 長恨 held by the National Museum of Korean Contemporary History (대한민국역사박물관),
published through e-Museum (e뮤지엄), operated by the National Museum of Korea.

Retrieved 16 September 2026. Both files are the front cover only, resampled to 900 × 1350 for
web delivery. Nothing else was changed: no crop, no rotation, no tonal adjustment. The museum
accession label visible at the foot of each photograph is part of the original image.

변경금지, that is, attribution required, no commercial use, no derivative works. The photographs are
therefore not covered by this project's CC BY 4.0 licence and must not be redistributed under it.
The 1927 magazine itself is out of copyright; the condition attaches to the museum's photograph.

The superseded files `jh01-cover.png` and `jh02-cover.png` are retained: the first is the 2005
digitisation of the 1927 original, the second the reproduction printed in 손종흠 외 편,
『근대 기생의 문화와 예술 자료편』 (보고사, 2009).
