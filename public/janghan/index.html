『長恨』 — the magazine the gisaeng made

Two issues appeared in 1927: 창간호 on 10 January and a February number. They were written largely by gisaeng, published by 長恨社 in Kyŏngsŏng, and edited in fact by the proletarian writer 최학송. This is a digital scholarly edition reconstructed from four independent witnesses.

This edition does not begin from a finished text. It begins from a disagreement: no two witnesses count the same number of articles. Reconstructing that disagreement is the scholarship.

The two issues

창간호 — January 1927

52 textual items in 48 editorial units. Attested by all four witnesses, including the original 1927 scan. Two-register page layout recovered.

2월호 — February 1927

46 textual items in 41 editorial units. No 1927 scan survives online, so the 2009 edition is the only text witness and page register is unknown throughout.

Editorial architecture

Symposium, round-table, serial fiction, recurring columns, nested poems — how the magazine was actually built.

Four witnesses

The 1927 original, the 2009 edition, the Yonsei catalogue, the published bibliography — and where they disagree.

What is being reconstructed

The magazine's contents have never been agreed. The published bibliography records 48 + 40 articles; the Yonsei catalogue lists 49 + 44; the 2009 reprint numbers its own sequence; the 1927 table of contents lists a curated selection. This edition holds those disagreements open at three levels — bibliographic entry, editorial unit, textual item — instead of resolving them silently.

Counting conventions differ mostly over poems and prompted features: a catalogue may list each poem separately while the magazine presents them inside a host article, and a single editorial prompt may carry three signed responses.
