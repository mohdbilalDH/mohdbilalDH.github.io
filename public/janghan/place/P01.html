京城 was the official Japanese-language name for Seoul 1906-1946. As the colonial administrative center, Keijō was where kwŏnbŏn regulation, Kyŏngsŏng-fu taxation, and the modern print industry were headquartered. The 1927 magazine is a Keijō-published object.
